Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your name, email address, and the expense data you choose to log. We do not collect sensitive financial identifiers like bank account numbers or credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service, generate your spending reports, and maintain your account security. We do not use your personal data for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your financial logs are stored securely and are only accessible by you through your authenticated account.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We only share data if required by law or to provide the core functionality of the app via trusted infrastructure providers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access the data you have logged and to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team through the application.
